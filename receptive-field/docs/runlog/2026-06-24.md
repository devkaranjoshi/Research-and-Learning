# Runlog — 2026-06-24

## [probe] Gradient sensitivity of 'it' to 'cat' across layers (coreference-flavored ERF)

- **Date:** 2026-06-24
- **Goal today:** Ad-hoc ERF probe — measure how much the 'cat' token influences the 'it' token's residual representation, across depth. Dial = layer index (rulers/model/prompt fixed).
- **Hypothesis:** Coreference resolves in mid/late layers (mech-interp lore; Elhage2021-style depth specialization), so gradient sensitivity of 'it' to its antecedent 'cat' should be LOW at the input and RISE with depth.
- **Experiment:** `experiments/probe_it_cat.py`. Prompt "cat sat on mat but it tired" (7 tokens: cat=0, sat=1, on=2, mat=3, but=4, it=5, tired=6). Ruler D (gradient), target = 'it' @ pos 5, layers {0,3,6,9,11}. Reported normalized influence of each prior token + rank of 'cat'.
- **Expected outcome:** cat→it influence increases with depth; cat climbs the ranking in mid layers.
- **Actual outcome:** cat→it influence 0.15 → 0.23 → 0.31 → 0.65 → 0.67 across layers 0/3/6/9/11. Rank of cat: 4th, 4th, **1st**, 2nd, 4th. Layer-9 ranking: sat 0.70 > cat 0.65 > mat 0.50 > but 0.31 > on 0.30. Layer 11: mat spikes to 1.00 and 'it' self-influence drops.
- **Surprises:** (1) cat is the *weakest* influence at layer 0 (furthest-back token, local early layers) yet becomes the *strongest* non-self influence by layer 6 — a clean depth-built link. (2) The model hedges the ambiguous pronoun: influence spreads over cat/sat/mat (S-V-O), soft preference for subject 'cat' (0.65) over object 'mat' (0.50), not a clean single-referent spike. (3) Final layer (11) is dominated by next-token-prediction pressure (mat→1.0), so mid-layers are the honest place to read semantic RF.
- **What failed:** —
- **What I learned:** The ERF/gradient ruler picks up a depth-localized coreference signal even on a 7-token toy sentence: pronoun→antecedent sensitivity is a mid-stack phenomenon. Confirms the layer-depth dependence flagged for T6, and that the final-layer residual is the wrong place to read token semantics.
- **Next step:** Compare with ruler A (attention weights) on the same token — does a specific head/layer point 'it'→'cat'? (T1 A-vs-D + a step toward T5 head taxonomy). Then resume the queued T11 attention-sinks experiment.
