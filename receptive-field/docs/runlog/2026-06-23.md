## [T9] FLAGSHIP — windowed effective RF vs depth: thesis confirmed, with a twist

- **Date:** 2026-06-23
- **Goal today:** Test the project thesis. Dial = attention pattern (full vs window=16 vs window=4), measured by gradient RF (ruler D) of the last token across layers {0,3,7,11}. 57-token prompt.
- **Hypothesis:** Beltagy2020 — stacked windowed layers grow theoretical reach to ~w×L (the conv recurrence). Luo2016 (by analogy) — but the *effective* reach should be ≪ theoretical, attenuating with each hop.
- **Experiment:** `experiments/t9_swa_erf.py`. Reach = farthest distance with influence > 1% of max. Compared observed reach to theoretical w×(L+1). Log-y plot of influence-vs-distance at layer 11 -> `docs/figures/t9_swa_erf.png`.
- **Expected outcome:** full reach=56 at all layers; windowed reach grows with depth; windowed < full.
- **Actual outcome:** Full reach = 56 at EVERY layer (global, as predicted). window=4 reach: 3→8→13→14 over layers 0/3/7/11 (monotonic growth with depth — depth law holds). window=16: 15→32→54→52 (nearly recovers full by layer 7). Plot: full ≈ flat ~0.1–1.0 across all distances; window=16 tracks full to ~15 then gentle decay, crosses 1% near dist 50; window=4 decays sharply and hits the structural-zero floor (1e-6) by dist ~28.
- **Surprises (the twist):** Effective reach is FAR below theoretical w×L. By layer 11, w=4 theory allows 48 but observed reach is only **14** (~30%); w=16 theory allows 192 but seq caps it. So depth grows reach *sublinearly* and it **saturates** — exactly the Luo2016 effective≪theoretical attenuation, now shown for windowed *attention*, not just convs. The w×L formula is an upper bound the network never reaches because each hop attenuates (visible as the green curve's steep log-linear decay).
- **What failed:** Nothing — clean run, exit 0.
- **What I learned:** The thesis holds in a sharper form: SWA approximates the *effective* RF, and effective reach ≈ (something like) sublinear-in-depth, well under the w×L theoretical bound. Practical reading: w=16 over 12 layers recovers ~all of GPT-2's effective RF (why moderate windows work); w=4 recovers only ~25% (too small → would lose long-range deps). This is the conv N5/N8 √depth result and the attention T9 result being literally the same phenomenon — strong material for the T14 synthesis.
- **Next step:** (a) **T11 attention sinks** — re-run w=4 with the first 1–2 tokens kept global (StreamingLLM patch) and show the severed long-range influence partially returns. (b) Quantify the gap as an explicit "approximation error" curve (full − window) for the DESIGN §3 table. (c) Cheap wins still open: T1 (A-vs-D divergence), T6 (reach vs layer for full attn — note it's already flat-global here).
