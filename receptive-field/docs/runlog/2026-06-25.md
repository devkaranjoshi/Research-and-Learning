# Runlog — 2026-06-25

## [T1/T4] it->cat head hunt — FALSE POSITIVE: it was the position-0 attention sink

- **Date:** 2026-06-25
- **Goal today:** Find the (layer,head) that points 'it' at 'cat' (ruler A), step toward T5 head taxonomy + T1 A-vs-D. Dial = ruler (A weights) on the it->cat pair; then adversarial controls.
- **Hypothesis:** A dedicated coreference head links the pronoun 'it' to its antecedent 'cat' (Clark2019 head specialization; mech-interp coreference lore).
- **Experiment:** `experiments/it_cat_head_hunt.py` on "cat sat on mat but it tired" — full 12x12 grid of A(it->cat). Then two controls: `it_cat_sink_control.py` (move cat off pos 0) and `it_cat_grad_control.py` (same control on ruler D).
- **Expected outcome:** one or a few heads with high A(it->cat) doing coreference.
- **Actual outcome:** 116/144 heads' argmax look from 'it' lands on 'cat' (top L5H1 = 0.996). TOO universal. Control: in "the dog saw a cat sat on mat but it tired" (cat @ pos4, 'the' @ pos0), heads with argmax='cat' collapse 116 -> 2, while argmax=pos0 stays 108/144; mean A(it->cat) 0.613 -> 0.036, A(it->pos0) stays 0.523. Gradient control: cat grad influence (L9) 0.649 -> 0.168 when moved off pos0, dropping cat to near-last rank, with pos0 token 'the' (0.190) above it.
- **Surprises:** BOTH rulers were contaminated. Ruler A loudly (sink artifact). Ruler D quietly — it is causally correct (pos-0 token genuinely carries influence as the sink), but attributing that to the WORD 'cat' vs the POSITION-0 role was an interpretation error. Neither ruler self-disambiguates word-identity from position-role.
- **What failed:** The original coreference hypothesis — no clear it->cat mechanism by either ruler on this sentence. The apparent signal was entirely GPT-2's position-0 attention sink (Xiao2023), because 'cat' happened to be the first token.
- **What I learned:** (1) T4 confirmed hard: attention weight != influence (sink eats 116 heads). (2) Deeper: even a causal gradient ruler needs a COUNTERFACTUAL CONTROL (move the token) to attribute influence to the right cause. The control, not the ruler, produces truth. (3) Always check whether a "found" antecedent sits at position 0 before claiming coreference. This reshapes T5/T11: the sink must be regressed out before any head-taxonomy claim.
- **Next step:** (a) Redo the head hunt with cat MID-sentence (cat not at pos0) so the sink can't masquerade — look for genuine it->cat heads above the pos-0 baseline. (b) T11 attention-sinks proper: quantify how much attention mass the pos-0 sink absorbs per layer, and re-run T9 window=4 with pos-0 kept global (StreamingLLM patch). (c) Add a "sink-subtracted" option to the ruler-A path in rf_probe.
